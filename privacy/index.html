Privacy policy

Your habit history stays on your device unless you choose to export it or your device backs it up. Tickfolio does not operate an account service or upload your habit or Health history to the developer.

Effective October 8, 2026 · Tickfolio for iPhone

Information stored in the app

Tickfolio stores the habits, goals, schedules, check-ins, measured values, notes, reminders, and preferences you create. These are used on your device to display your habits, calculate statistics, show widgets, and schedule local reminders. The app and its widget extension share local storage. There is no required account, advertising, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking.

Optional Apple Health access

If you connect a habit to Apple Health, Tickfolio asks permission to read the selected measurement: steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes, or sleep. It uses that data to calculate daily progress and stores daily readings locally so that history, statistics, and widgets can work. It does not write to Apple Health.

Health data is not sent to the developer, sold, used for advertising, or shared with data brokers. Manual tracking works without Health permission. You can change access in Apple Health or your iPhone’s Settings. Turning off access stops new authorized reads; it does not automatically erase readings already saved in Tickfolio. Delete the linked habit or all habits to remove its saved history. This does not change the original Apple Health records.

Widgets and reminders

Widgets display information from your local habit history on the surfaces where you add them. Reminders may show habit names in notifications. You control widget placement, notification previews, and notification permissions through iOS settings.

Backups and exports

You can export habit backups and CSV files to a destination you choose. Saved Apple Health history is included only when you turn on Include Apple Health history for export. Exported files may contain sensitive information; the service or person you choose to share them with receives that information under their own privacy practices.

Tickfolio does not provide automatic cloud sync. Your device’s backup service may include app data according to your Apple and device settings. The developer cannot access those backups. Imported Health history is stored locally; reconnect Health only if you want new automatic readings.

Retention and deletion

Habit data stays in the app until you delete it. Delete one habit or use Settings & data → Delete all habits to remove habits and their saved Health history. Deleting Tickfolio from your device removes its local app data; offloading the app preserves data. Exported files, device backups, and original Apple Health records are separate and must be managed where they are stored. Restoring a backup may restore previously deleted history.

Contacting support

If you email tickfolio.support@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include. This is used to respond and troubleshoot your request. Support email is handled through Gmail and its service providers. Please do not send Health records, habit backups, passwords, or other sensitive information unless a specific issue makes sharing necessary and you choose to do so.

Support messages are kept as needed to address the request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can request deletion by emailing the same address.

Apple services and this website

Apple processes App Store, device backup, and diagnostic information under its own terms and your settings. If you use TestFlight, Apple may provide the developer with beta feedback and diagnostic information to improve the app.

Opening this support website makes a normal web request. The hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details to serve and protect the website. This website does not receive your habit or Health history, and we do not add advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies.

Changes and questions

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or support-message deletion requests, contact tickfolio.support@gmail.com.
