Common questions

How do I create or edit a habit?

Tap + on the home screen and choose Yes or No or Measurable. Set your goal and frequency, then save. To edit a habit, tap its name to open statistics and use the pencil button.

How does Apple Health tracking work?

Choose Apple Health under Tracking when creating or editing a habit. Connect steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes, or sleep, and allow access to that measurement. Tickfolio reads Health data; it does not write to Apple Health. You can also use Tickfolio with manual entries only.

If new readings are missing, check that data exists in the Health app and review Tickfolio’s Health permissions. Then open Settings & data → Apple Health → Refresh Apple Health. Previously saved readings can remain after permission is turned off.

How do I edit an earlier day?

Swipe horizontally across the dates on the home screen to go back in time. In a habit’s statistics, choose Calendar → Edit. Tap a yes-or-no date to cycle its state, or a measurable date to enter a value. A manual edit takes priority over automatic Health tracking for that day; “Use Apple Health again” restores automatic tracking while the habit is connected.

How do I add widgets?

Touch and hold an empty part of your iPhone Home Screen and open the widget gallery. Search for Tickfolio, choose a widget, and add it. Touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget to select a habit. The Checkmark widget can update a yes-or-no habit; entering a measured value opens the app.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Edit the habit and turn on its reminder, then select the time and days. Allow notifications for Tickfolio in your iPhone’s Settings. Also check whether Focus or your notification settings are silencing alerts.

How do I back up or move my habits?

Open Settings & data → Export backup, and save the file somewhere you can find again. Import backup adds copies of the saved habits without replacing your existing habits. CSV export is for viewing history in a spreadsheet. Turn on Include Apple Health history before exporting if you want saved Health readings included. After restoring, reconnect Apple Health to resume automatic updates. There is no automatic sync between devices.

How do I delete my data?

Delete an individual habit from the selection menu, or use Delete all habits in Settings & data to remove your habits and their saved Health history. This does not delete the original records in Apple Health. Exported files and device backups are separate copies; remove those from their storage locations if you no longer want them.
